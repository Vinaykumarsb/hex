Three moves, no new app to learn

  1. Hold the shortcut

    Ctrl + Space on Windows, Option on a Mac. You can change it in General.

  2. Speak normally

    Full sentences, part numbers, names. A small indicator at the top of the screen shows it's listening.

  3. Let go

    The text lands where your cursor is. Tap twice for hands-free, then press once to finish.

Built to stay on your desk

On-device

Your voice never leaves the laptop

The speech model runs locally. After a one-time model download, dictation works with Wi-Fi off.

No audio kept

Recordings are discarded

Audio is thrown away once it's transcribed. Only the text is kept in History, for 7 days, on your computer.

Any app

Types where you're working

It pastes like a keyboard would, so Outlook, Teams, SAP, browsers, and Word all work without plug-ins.

Many languages

Speak the language you write in

English, German, French, Spanish, Polish, Chinese, Japanese and many more. Pick your language and speech model in General.

Install in two minutes

Windows 10 and 11

  1. Run nexus-voice-setup-…-windows-x64.exe.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow setup. It downloads the speech model once.
  4. Find it in the tray by the clock. Right-click for the menu.

Mac with Apple silicon

  1. Open the DMG and drag Nexus Voice to Applications.
  2. Open it. If macOS can't check it, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  3. Allow Microphone and Accessibility so it can type for you.
  4. It lives in the menu bar.
