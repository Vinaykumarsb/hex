# Install Nexus Voice

Nexus Voice turns your voice into text in any app: Outlook, Teams, Word,
your browser, anything with a text field. It runs entirely on your computer;
nothing you say is sent anywhere.

Download the latest version from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/Vinaykumarsb/hex/releases/latest).

## Windows

1. Download `nexus-voice-setup-…-windows-x64.exe` and run it.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info → Run
   anyway**. It's a one-time warning for apps that aren't from the Store.
3. Nexus Voice opens and walks you through setup. It downloads its speech
   model once, so the first start takes a few minutes.
4. Allow microphone access if Windows asks
   (Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone).

Nexus Voice lives in the system tray, by the clock. Right-click its icon for
the menu.

## Mac (Apple silicon)

1. Download `nexus-voice-…-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag
   **Nexus Voice** to **Applications**.
2. Open it from Applications. If macOS says it can't check the app,
   open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
3. Follow setup: allow **Microphone** and **Accessibility** (to type into
   other apps). It then downloads its speech model once.

Nexus Voice lives in the menu bar.

## Use it

- **Hold** the shortcut (Option on Mac, Ctrl+Space on Windows; change it
  on the General tab), **speak**, then **let go**. Your words
  appear where your cursor is.
- **Tap twice** to keep recording hands-free; press once more to finish.
- Paste the last dictation again: Option-Shift-V (Mac).
- Change the shortcut, microphone, or language under **General**.
- Past dictations are under **History** (kept 7 days, only on your computer).

## Updates

Nexus Voice doesn't update itself. When a new version is announced, download
it from the Releases page and install it over the old one; your settings and
History stay.
